About OpenShelf

A cleaner way to discover software.

OpenShelf is designed as a modern software discovery platform: fast search, useful categories, detailed app pages and clear source labels. The goal is to make it obvious whether a listing points to an official publisher page or a community-provided release.

SimpleNo account is required just to browse the directory.
TransparentSource type and platform information are shown near each app.
ExtensibleThe static structure can later connect to a database, analytics, ads, reviews or an admin panel.
